Bubbles OS utilizes the following! :

  • 7.css the Windows 7 buttons, windows and scrollbars (MIT)
  • WinBox.js makes the windows drag, resize and stack (Apache 2.0)
  • Boxicons the smaller interface glyphs dotted around (MIT)
  • Frutiger Aero Archive the wallpaper, the Vista icons, and honestly the whole vibe

Bubbles is a free desktop template. You do not have to credit the template itself, but please keep the four above. They are the ones doing the heavy lifting! ^_^

Welcome! Here's how to get around:

  • Click a desktop icon to open a window.
  • Drag a title bar to move a window, or drag its edges to resize.
  • Windows fill the screen here, close one to get back to the desktop.
  • Use the taskbar to switch or minimize windows.
  • Hit Start for the full menu.
